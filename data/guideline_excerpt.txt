GUIDELINE EXCERPT: CPIC 2022 update, CYP2C19 genotype and clopidogrel therapy

Allele function: *1 = normal function; *2 and *3 = no function; *9 = decreased function; *17 = increased function. Any other allele (for example *12) has uncertain function.

How a diplotype becomes a phenotype:
- two normal-function alleles: Normal metabolizer
- one normal-function + one increased-function allele: Rapid metabolizer
- two increased-function alleles: Ultrarapid metabolizer
- one normal-function + one no-function allele, OR one increased-function + one no-function allele: Intermediate metabolizer
- two no-function alleles: Poor metabolizer
- one normal + one decreased, OR one increased + one decreased, OR two decreased: Likely intermediate metabolizer
- one decreased + one no-function allele: Likely poor metabolizer
- one or two uncertain-function alleles: Indeterminate (the recommendation tables below contain no recommendation for it)

A "Likely" phenotype gets the same recommendation and strength as the corresponding confirmed phenotype (Likely intermediate = Intermediate; Likely poor = Poor).

Recommendations when considering clopidogrel, by indication:
Indication: ACS/PCI
- Ultrarapid metabolizer: If considering clopidogrel, use at standard dose (75 mg/day) (strength: Strong)
- Rapid metabolizer: If considering clopidogrel, use at standard dose (75 mg/day) (strength: Strong)
- Normal metabolizer: If considering clopidogrel, use at standard dose (75 mg/day) (strength: Strong)
- Intermediate metabolizer: Avoid standard dose (75 mg) clopidogrel if possible. Use prasugrel or ticagrelor at standard dose if no contraindication. (strength: Strong)
- Poor metabolizer: Avoid clopidogrel if possible. Use prasugrel or ticagrelor at standard dose if no contraindication. (strength: Strong)

Indication: non-ACS non-PCI cardiovascular
- Ultrarapid metabolizer: No recommendation (strength: No recommendation)
- Rapid metabolizer: No recommendation (strength: No recommendation)
- Normal metabolizer: If considering clopidogrel, use at standard dose (75 mg/day) (strength: Strong)
- Intermediate metabolizer: No recommendation (strength: No recommendation)
- Poor metabolizer: Avoid clopidogrel if possible. Use prasugrel or ticagrelor at standard dose if no contraindication. (strength: Moderate)

Indication: neurovascular
- Ultrarapid metabolizer: No recommendation (strength: No recommendation)
- Rapid metabolizer: No recommendation (strength: No recommendation)
- Normal metabolizer: If considering clopidogrel, use at standard dose (75 mg/day) (strength: Strong)
- Intermediate metabolizer: Consider an alternative P2Y12 inhibitor at standard dose if clinically indicated and no contraindication. (strength: Moderate)
- Poor metabolizer: Avoid clopidogrel if possible. Consider an alternative P2Y12 inhibitor at standard dose if clinically indicated and no contraindication. (strength: Moderate)
